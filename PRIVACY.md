# Privacy Notice

PocketRelay runs locally on your computer.

- Project source code, credentials, and databases remain on your local machine.
- Your data does not pass through PocketRelay servers.
- The only external services involved are Telegram (for the bot interface) and Google (if using Google Antigravity).
- Telemetry is disabled by default.
- You can configure the application to not store prompts, responses, and diffs locally.
